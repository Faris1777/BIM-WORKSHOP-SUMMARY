Why Early Tendering?

  • ALEC is involved in Early Tendering of Mega Projects Designed by Master Developers.
  • Master Developers avoid developing Full IFC without knowing the COST.
  • Master Developers Hires ALEC to calculate the COST of project, to know how much it will cost before proceeding with developing full IFC.
  • Early Tendering is Fast Reality Check for Owner to know if he should proceed with Full IFC or Not.

Why does ALEC need all these departments and Consultants for Tendering?

  • Estimation Department to calculate the COST.
  • Planning Department for making the PRELIMINARY SCHEDULE.
  • Commercial Department for allocating the RISK.
  • Consultants for filling the gaps due to the INCOMPLETE DESIGN.

What defines Boreholes Number, Location & Depth?

Eurocode 7 — EN 1997 1 & 2

Circular 350

  • G+12: 1 borehole per 750m², minimum 5 boreholes.
  • Less than G+12: 1 borehole per 750m², minimum 3 boreholes.
  • Large Structures: Grid of 60 meters between boreholes.
  • Single Villa: Minimum 2 boreholes per villa.
  • Compound Villa: 1 borehole per plot.
  • Depth: To be calculated utilizing Eurocode geotechnical design.

Design Flow

  • Concept
    What are we trying to build? It shows what the building is, massing, height, how big it is, and how it sits in a plot.
  • Schematic
    Translates concept into testable system. Structural grids, columns layout, dimension plans... Soil report is active now, fire strategy.
  • Detailed design "IFC"
    Fully engineered build. At this stage we have full structural calculations, load combinations, sections, and approved drawings.

Sequence Of Foundation Works

Shoring

Piling

Dewatering

Excavation

  • There is no specific order for foundation in any code in the world.
  • GIFR & GIR
  • The correct sequence of foundation works is chosen based on results from GIR.
  • The sequence of foundation works may vary depending on the structure, and its location:
    • Coastal Areas: First dewatering, shoring, then excavation, and finally piling.
    • Deep Basement: Shoring first, dewatering, excavation, and finally piling.
    • Very Deep Basement (Tight Site): Piles first, shoring, dewatering, and excavation.
    • No Basement (Shallow): Piling, excavation and dewatering, and shoring if required.
